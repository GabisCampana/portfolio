About gabriela

I run operations for founders and CEOs at venture-backed startups, US ecommerce brands, and DevOps consultancies.

I'm an Operations and Technical Programs leader with 10 years of experience delivering complex programs and operations at companies that include Google, F.biz (WPP), Cloud Posse, and Brooklyn Jewelers.

Most recently, I was hired as Chief Operating Officer at Brooklyn Jewelers in New York. The engagement is currently on pause due to the founder's family circumstances, so I'm exploring my next COO or Head of Operations role.

Before BKJ, I spent three years at Cloud Posse, an AWS Premier Partner DevOps consultancy serving venture-backed and enterprise clients on AWS. I was the sole Technical Project Manager, reporting directly to the CEO with cross-functional ownership across delivery, vendor partnerships, AWS Partner Network, and operating cadence. I led the company's migration from Jira to Linear and ran client delivery across multiple concurrent engagements.

Earlier, I led HexClad Cookware's site redesign at Vaan and FLEO's headless commerce migration at The Form Factory. I started my career at Google Brazil, where I led production of "All Brazil Plays" for the Rio 2016 Olympics, coordinating Google Brazil, the production agency MediaMonks (Netherlands), and the US-based management team.

I hold a Bachelor's in Communication and Advertising from Universidade de São Paulo, with coursework in Digital Media at the University of New South Wales.

How I work

I report directly to founders and CEOs, and I operate as the connective tissue between strategy and execution.

What that looks like in practice:
- I run weekly operating cadences (delivery reviews, capacity planning, escalations) so leadership stays out of the weeds.
- I own program ownership end-to-end: scoping, staffing, vendor management, milestone tracking, and outcomes.
- I build SOPs and operating systems that scale beyond me, not playbooks that depend on me.
- I work autonomously and cross-functionally across product, engineering, design, and customer-facing teams.

I'm direct, results-driven, and disciplined. I work fully remote across timezones, with a strong preference for written-first, async-friendly cultures.
