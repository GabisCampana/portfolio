About

I run operations for founders and CEOs who need someone to own the whole machine.

13 years delivering complex programs across Google, Cloud Posse, Brooklyn Jewelers, and a handful of US ecommerce brands. I'm the connective tissue between strategy and execution.

I'm an Operations and Technical Programs leader with 13 years of experience delivering complex programs at companies that include Google, Cloud Posse, and Brooklyn Jewelers.

Most recently, I was hired as Chief Operating Officer at Brooklyn Jewelers in New York. The engagement is currently on pause due to the founder's family circumstances, so I'm exploring my next COO or Head of Operations role.

Before BKJ, I spent three years at Cloud Posse, an AWS Premier Partner DevOps consultancy. I was the sole Technical Project Manager, reporting directly to the CEO with cross-functional ownership across delivery, vendor partnerships, the AWS Partner Network, and operating cadence. I led the company's migration from Jira to Linear and ran client delivery across multiple concurrent engagements.

Earlier, I led HexClad Cookware's site redesign at Vaan and FLEO's headless commerce migration at The Form Factory. I started my career at Google Brazil, where I led production of "All Brazil Plays" for the Rio 2016 Olympics, coordinating Google Brazil, MediaMonks (Netherlands), and the US-based management team.

I hold a Bachelor's in Communication and Advertising from Universidade de São Paulo, with coursework in Digital Media at the University of New South Wales.
