Context

Branded digital ops for the most-watched Olympics in history.

For the Rio 2016 Olympics, Google sold a branded content platform to 4 major Brazilian brands. "All of Brazil Plays" (O Brasil Inteiro Joga) was a personalized feed of original Rio content — daily videos and 42 custom-designed minigames — where each user's experience was tailored to their interests and continued across programmatic banners on the web.

As Interactive Producer, I was embedded in the operations: keeping the platform running before, during, and after the Games — managing partners, QA-ing builds, publishing content, and running live production at scale.

Case Video
Operations & Partner Management

Running the business side of a multi-brand platform

With 4 major Brazilian brands as stakeholders, keeping everyone aligned required tight operational control. I owned scheduling, reporting, financials, and risk — making sure the platform delivered against every client commitment.

  • Managed partner relationships across 4 Brazilian brands throughout the full engagement
  • Owned GanttChart scheduling and milestone tracking for the project timeline
  • Processed POs and invoices — financial accountability from kickoff to post-launch
  • Risk management across a live digital platform operating during a global sporting event
  • Tracking and reporting across all workstreams and brand commitments
Content & Platform Operations

Keeping a live personalized platform stocked and performing

The platform ran on daily content — videos, games, and personalized feeds. I managed the content pipeline, taxonomy, and publishing systems to keep the experience fresh throughout the full Games window.

  • Content management and daily publishing across the personalized feed
  • Taxonomy and categorization for content surfacing and interest-based personalization
  • SEO implementation and ongoing optimization for platform discoverability
  • Coordinated with the dev team to surface and resolve content-related issues in real time
QA & Live Production

Zero tolerance for errors on a live Olympics platform

With millions of users watching the Games, there was no room for bugs or broken experiences. I led QA across UI, UX, and development, managed sprint cycles, and ran live production throughout the full event window.

  • QA across UI, UX, and development — reviewed and approved each build before release
  • Managed project sprints and development follow-up throughout the full engagement
  • Feedbacks and approvals coordinated across client, design, and engineering
  • Live production operations for the full duration of the Rio 2016 Games
