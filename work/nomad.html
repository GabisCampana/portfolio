Stakeholder & Backlog Management

Turning a chaotic backlog into a focused roadmap

Nomad's PM came with a long, unranked list of requests that spanned UX improvements, new features, performance fixes, and integrations. My first job was to bring structure: work with their team to understand business priority, translate that into a ranked backlog, and give the dev team a clear sprint-by-sprint execution plan.

I ran weekly calls with Nomad's internal PM to review progress, surface blockers, and align on upcoming priorities. This created a feedback loop that kept both teams aligned and reduced the back-and-forth that typically eats into delivery time.

  • Led weekly backlog grooming sessions with Nomad's product team
  • Prioritized features by conversion impact and implementation effort
  • Maintained sprint plans across a multi-developer team
  • Translated client priorities into engineering-ready acceptance criteria

Performance Outcomes

Results that showed up in the numbers

Over the 5-month engagement, the focused execution against a properly prioritized backlog produced measurable outcomes across Nomad's core e-commerce metrics. A +25% improvement in conversion rate and a -15.6% reduction in bounce rate weren't accidents — they were the result of disciplined prioritization and consistent delivery.

By keeping the team aligned on high-impact work and reducing churn from unplanned scope changes, we were able to ship meaningful improvements every sprint rather than getting stuck in endless planning cycles.

  • +25% improvement in e-commerce conversion rate over the engagement period
  • -15.6% reduction in bounce rate through targeted UX and performance improvements
  • Consistent sprint delivery with no major delays or scope overruns
  • Strengthened the agency-client relationship, creating the foundation for continued work
